## 1. Overall Design Direction

Our preferred interior style is **Modern Luxury**, using a combination of:

- Beige and cream colour palettes.
- Natural wood finishes.
- Subtle wall textures and decorative panels.
- Warm ambient and indirect LED lighting.
- Minimal, elegant furniture and practical storage solutions.

The objective is to achieve a premium, spacious and elegant appearance without overcrowding the rooms or introducing unnecessary furniture.

**Important:** The original apartment floor plan must be preserved. All room positions, walls, doors, windows, bathrooms, balconies and the main entrance must remain consistent with the actual 2D architectural layout.
